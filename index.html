      How it works

      From word list to printed page in four steps

      1. 1

        Add your words

        Type or paste a list, or pick a theme and let the generator choose. Spaces are removed, so ICE CREAM hides as ICECREAM.

      2. 2

        Set the rules

        Choose which of the eight directions words can run, how often they cross, and how large the grid is.

      3. 3

        Shape the grid

        Pick a heart, star or ring, turn a letter or an image into a shape, or paint the cells yourself.

      4. 4

        Export the page

        Set the paper size and resolution, add a title and word list, and save a JPG or a PDF. Choose Solution or Both to get the answer key too.

      Features

      Control over every part of the puzzle

      8 directions

      Choose how words run

      Tick any mix of horizontal, vertical, diagonal and backwards directions. Fewer directions suit younger solvers. All eight make a hard puzzle.

      4 crossing levels

      Decide how often words cross

      None keeps every word on its own cells. High packs words so they cross as often as they can, which also helps fit more words into a small grid.

      10 shapes

      Shapes and masks

      Start from a preset, or build a mask from a letter, an image or by painting cells. Words always stay inside the outline.

      Up to 600 dpi

      Print-ready export

      Save A4, Letter, square or custom sizes in millimetres, inches or pixels, with a live pixel count and a quality slider. Save a JPG, or a multi-page PDF of several puzzles.

      6 word themes

      Random word lists

      Animals, space, food, nature, ocean and tech, with minimum and maximum lengths so every word suits your grid.

      Answer key

      Solution overlay

      Show every word as a neat colour-coded highlight, then include it in the export when you want a teacher's copy.

      FAQ

      Questions people ask

      Do I need an account?

      There is nothing to sign up for. Open the page, build a puzzle and export it.

      Do my words or images leave my device?

      No. Your word list, images and shapes are processed in your browser. The only files created are the JPG or PDF you export, and each is saved only after you confirm the download. Saved puzzles stay in your own browser storage on this device.

      How do I choose which directions words can run?

      Use the compass in the Rules tab of the sidebar. Each arrow shows the way a word is read, so the left, up and upward-diagonal arrows place words backwards or upwards. The Quick set menu switches between common combinations, such as easy puzzles that only read left to right and top to bottom.

      What do the crossing levels mean?

      None means words never share a letter. Low allows rare crossings. Medium lets words cross when they happen to meet. High packs words so they cross as often as they can. The status line under the grid shows how many crossings your puzzle has.

      Why did one of my words not fit?

      A word may be longer than the grid or the shape allows, or the enabled directions may have no room left. Try a larger grid, more directions or a higher crossing level. The words that did not fit are listed under the grid so you can see which ones to change.

      How do I make a heart, a star or my own shape?

      Open the Shape tab in the sidebar and pick a preset. To go further, type a letter or a short word, choose an image, or open the shape editor and paint cells by hand. Dark areas of an image become playable cells, and the light areas switch reverses that.

      What size should I export for printing?

      A4 at 300 dpi is 2480 × 3508 pixels and US Letter at 300 dpi is 2550 × 3300 pixels. 150 dpi is enough for most home printers and keeps the file small. The exact pixel size is shown above the Export button.

      How do I make an answer key?

      Switch on Show solution to check the puzzle. In the Export tab choose Solution or Both. A JPG saves the puzzle and the answer key as two files, the answer key with "-solution" in its name. A PDF puts every puzzle first, then a Solutions page, then every answer.

      How do I make a PDF with several puzzles?

      Build a puzzle, open the Saved tab and press Save. Repeat for each puzzle. Tick the ones you want, then press Export as PDF. Use the arrows to set the order. The PDF has all the puzzles first, a Solutions divider page, and then every answer page in the same order.

      Which characters can my words contain?

      Letters only. Spaces, digits and punctuation are removed, and accents are stripped, so Crème brûlée becomes CREMEBRULEE. Each word needs at least two letters, and duplicates are removed automatically.

      Does it save my puzzle?

      Not between visits. Export the JPG to keep a puzzle. Shuffle layout creates a new arrangement of the same words whenever you want one.
